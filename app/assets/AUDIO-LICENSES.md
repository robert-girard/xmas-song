# Bundled audio rights

The APK includes the exact local Ogg files listed below. The composition and
the recording/arrangement are documented separately because they have distinct
rights. The credits are retained in the app package even where the recording
license does not require attribution.

## Track 01 — Jingle Bells (Festival speech synthesis)

- **Composition:** “Jingle Bells,” James Lord Pierpont (1857), public domain.
- **Recording / arrangement:** Robotchoir, own work: the Festival Speech
  Synthesis System singing mode with its default US voice and piano
  accompaniment by TiMidity/FreePats. Robotchoir released the recording into
  the public domain worldwide. The source page states that, where a public
  domain dedication is not legally possible, anyone may use the work for any
  purpose without conditions unless required by law. FreePats describes its
  sample-license policy as permitting musical works to be distributed under a
  license chosen by the author ([FreePats project](https://freepats.zenvoid.org/about.html)).
- **Exact source:** [Wikimedia Commons file page](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jingle_Bells_Or_The_One_Horse_Open_Sleigh_Complete.ogg).
- **Original file:** [Wikimedia upload](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/32/Jingle_Bells_Or_The_One_Horse_Open_Sleigh_Complete.ogg).
- **Attribution:** Robotchoir, “Jingle Bells Or The One Horse Open Sleigh
  Complete,” Wikimedia Commons; public-domain dedication. Courtesy credit is
  retained although the dedication has no attribution condition.
- **Verification date:** 2026-11-26. The Commons page identifies Robotchoir as
  the author and describes the Festival singing-mode performance and TiMidity /
  FreePats accompaniment. Its structured metadata gives a SHA-1 of
  `0fbad23b102ca85c2ccdb2b6638442d9554e9ed6` and duration of 146.0070748 seconds.
- **Packaged file:** `android/app/src/main/res/raw/track_01.ogg`; exact original
  download, untrimmed and untranscoded.
- **Duration:** 146.007 seconds.
- **Size:** 2,086,958 bytes.
- **SHA-256:** `9cef62c273dce5f05c8eacc8ddd6b6ba49544f90da29f284708074014d115599`.

The composition is public domain independently of the recording and its
arrangement. The recording's worldwide public-domain release (with the stated
unrestricted fallback) is the basis for redistributing this exact file.

## Track 02 — Deck the Halls (home-recorded bells)

- **Composition:** “Deck the Halls,” a traditional carol based on the Welsh
  tune “Nos Galan”; Hymnary identifies the Welsh dance tune as public domain.
  [Tune record](https://www.hymnary.org/tune/nos_galan_welsh).
- **Recording / arrangement:** Ihaksi recorded bells at home and arranged them
  into a short “Deck The Halls” melody. The Freesound sound page publishes the
  recording under Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal. That dedication permits
  copying, modification, distribution, and performance without requiring
  attribution.
- **Exact source:** [Freesound sound 411420](https://freesound.org/people/Ihaksi/sounds/411420/).
- **Packaged source file:** [Freesound low-quality Ogg preview](https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/411/411420_6014995-lq.ogg), the exact preview URL published by the source page; it is not edited or transcoded locally. The page's downloadable master is WAV; this package uses the already encoded Ogg preview to keep the asset small.
- **Attribution:** Ihaksi, “Deck The Halls - Christmas jingle played with
  bells,” Freesound sound 411420, CC0 1.0. Attribution is retained as a
  courtesy; CC0 does not require it.
- **Verification date:** 2026-11-26. The source page identifies Ihaksi,
  describes the creator's own bell samples and short melody, and marks the
  sound CC0. Source-page duration is 7.594 seconds, 44.1 kHz stereo.
- **Changes:** No local edits or transcoding. The Ogg preview is packaged as-is.
- **Packaged file:** `android/app/src/main/res/raw/track_02.ogg`.
- **Duration:** 7.5949433 seconds (decoded packaged Ogg; source page reports
  7.594 seconds for its sound).
- **Size:** 61,810 bytes.
- **SHA-256:** `197a340753f5584b94b02485e644e5877edf28309edc6166eaf6aa4a7162a174`.

The traditional composition and this exact home-recorded bell performance
have separate rights bases. CC0 covers the sound recording and its arrangement;
the composition is public domain.
